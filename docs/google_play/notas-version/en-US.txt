What's new

A PDF you open from a messaging app or your file manager is no longer read-only. The viewer now has a toolbox to sign it, edit it, protect it with a password, split or export it, and save it wherever you want, without leaving and hunting for the file again.

• Sharing several PDFs at once takes you to merging them.
• Sharing photos takes you to turning them into a document.
• Opening a backup offers to import it.
